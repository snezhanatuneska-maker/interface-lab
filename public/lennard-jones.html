Lennard-Jones Potential Explorer

Tune ε and σ to see the potential well and the force between two neutral atoms or molecules.

Controls

Clean energy link

The molecular basis of adhesion, adsorption and cohesion. The well depth ε sets how strongly a gas molecule binds to a surface (physisorption heat, hence Langmuir and BET uptake), and how strongly liquids hold together (cohesion). σ sets the size of the molecule, which controls how many fit on an adsorbent surface. In H2 storage and CO2 capture materials, a deeper well means stronger binding at a given temperature.

Hard-sphere limit: as n grows the repulsive wall becomes a vertical line at r ≈ σ, so the molecule behaves like a rigid sphere with a weak attractive tail.
